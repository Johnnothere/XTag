Method and limits. Figures reflect only what was reachable at generation time; a source that was rate-limited, blocked or slow is absent, and absence here is not evidence of absence in the world. Narrative clusters, entities, relationships and the executive brief are produced by a language model from collected text and are unverified assertions, not findings. Sentiment is a machine classification; documents that could not be scored are reported as unscored rather than counted as neutral. Coordination and inauthenticity are campaign-shape measurements over observable text and metadata — they make no claim about any individual account being automated, and account age, follower graphs and posting history are not available from these sources. The physical-security dimension is a lexical screen that flags language for human review; it does not assess intent, capability or credibility, and must not be read as a prediction of violence. Where the evidence base was too thin to support a judgement, the band reads "unknown" — that means the assessment could not be made, not that nothing is happening.
