02  Capabilities

See what XTag can do.

16+ live sources

Social, news, state media, Telegram, GDELT, academic and podcasts — fired in parallel, per query.

Query expansion

Script variants, digit forms and written surface shapes — a tag folded three ways still counts as one tag.

The relevance gate

Every document scored for relevance before anything else is computed; dropped documents kept for audit.

Engagement, not views

Reactions, comments and shares are acts; impressions are not. XTag never mixes the two.

Coordination clusters

Actor–trait graph over shared links, hashtags, wording and timing — validated against a red-team harness.

Captured vs manufactured reach

The operation's own noise, split from the audience it genuinely won.

Velocity & propagation

Acceleration windows and first-seen platform ordering, computed over the whole corpus.

Global tone & volume

News tone, article volume in σ above baseline, source-country concentration and audience languages.

Narratives & entities

Recurring frames with their claims, actors and documents; every extracted quote verified against the corpus.

Amplification map

Which narrative seeded which, which moved as a set — and which never reached the public domain.

Identity over time

Births, growth on share, drift and deaths — the same story recognised week to week.

Written brief & dossier

A defensible written verdict, and a print-first dossier where every missing section says why.

Confidence, gated

Volume, purity, source health and recency each gate the number — no amount of coverage escapes thin evidence.

Unknown is not low

Below eight documents or a quarter of signal, the band is "unknown" — a dash, never a green light.

Degradation, named

A failed source is excluded and named; a degraded run is returned honestly and never cached as fact.

Fenced from the evidence

The corpus is adversarial by definition — instructions inside it are findings to analyse, never orders to obey.

Watchlists & alerts

Saved queries re-evaluated fresh on schedule; alerts only on genuine movement, never on collection size.

Email reports

Scheduled digests with deltas since last delivery — honest about what was not assessed.

Cases, kept locally

Every search is a case, stored in your browser — reopen it instantly, nothing re-queried, nothing spent.

Time series

Every check persisted as an observation, so trends are measured against records, not memory.

04  Questions

Asked often.

Sixteen-plus live sources per query: public social platforms, news via the GDELT global index, a curated state-media feed list, Telegram channels, academic indexes and podcasts. Everything is collected at query time, deduplicated, and screened for relevance before anything is scored.

It shows you the structure: which accounts move together, how cohesive the cluster is, how much of the reach the operation generated itself, and which narratives it fed. Attribution to a sponsor is an analyst's judgment — XTag deliberately does not automate it, because a tool that guesses attribution is a tool you cannot cite.

A narrative that is present in the collection but absent from high-credibility news — pushed, sometimes hard, yet it never reached the public domain in that pull. Often that is the most interesting finding on the page: an operation that has not broken out yet. A wider or later collection may show a breakout, and the report says so.

It is named, excluded from coverage, and the assessment renormalises over what remains — a missing signal is never scored as zero. If too little survives, the verdict becomes "unknown" rather than "low", and a degraded run is never cached as if it were a complete one.

Arabic, Persian, Hebrew and English, scored natively in each language — no translate-then-analyse detour. Queries are expanded into script and surface variants so the same tag written three ways still counts as one tag. Translations are produced for display only.

First evidence is on screen in seconds — collection and relevance screening run ahead of the deeper analysis, which streams in behind it. A full multi-stage assessment, including coordination, narrative extraction and the scored verdict, completes within a few minutes at most.
